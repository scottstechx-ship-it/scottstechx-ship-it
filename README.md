### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm a Ugandan cybersecurity analyst turned full-stack engineer and founder of **ScottsTechX Enterprise (U) Ltd**. I build trust-gated marketplaces, AI-powered threat detection, and Progressive Web Apps — designed for low-bandwidth, mobile-first, real-world Uganda, then hardened for production.

- 🔭 Currently leading **Commerce OS** — a marketplace platform with a Fastify/Postgres backend and a Kotlin/Compose Android client.
- 🌱 Building **Sentinel AI** — AI threat-detection reference architecture.
- 💼 Open for commissioned engineering, security audits, and AI consulting.
- 📫 Reach me at **scottsstechx@gmail.com**
